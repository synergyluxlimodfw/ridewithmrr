FAQ

Common questions, answered.

How do I get an exact price? +
Send your trip details through the booking form or text Mr. R at (646) 879-1391. You'll get a confirmed flat rate, usually within minutes.
How far in advance should I book? +
At least 24 hours for standard rides. For weddings, proms, and major events, book 2–4 weeks ahead. Same-day bookings welcomed based on availability — call (646) 879-1391 directly.
Do you track flights for airport pickups? +
Yes — we track every flight in real time. If your flight is delayed or arrives early, we adjust automatically. 60 minutes free wait on international, 30 minutes on domestic arrivals.
Is Ride with Mr. R owner-operated? +
Yes — 100%. Ride with Mr. R is owner-operated by Mr. Rodriguez. Every chauffeur is hand-picked and trained to his standard — no dispatched strangers, no unknown drivers. You might ride with Mr. R himself, or with someone he trusts to do it his way.
What forms of payment do you accept? +
Once Mr. R confirms your ride, you'll receive a secure payment link by text or email. We accept all major cards, Apple Pay, Google Pay, and Zelle. A 50% deposit secures your date for weddings, proms, and holiday bookings.
What is your cancellation policy? +
Standard bookings require 48 hours notice for a full refund. Wedding and prom packages require 7 days. New Year's Eve and major holiday bookings are non-refundable once confirmed.
¿Hablan español? +
Sí. Puede llamar, escribir o reservar su viaje en español. Mr. R es bilingüe.
